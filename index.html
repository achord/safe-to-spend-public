What makes it different

  • It measures you, not an average. The everyday buffer comes from your real spending history.
  • It admits what it doesn't know. Variable bills are flagged rather than guessed at; dates are stated plainly because dates are the reliable part.
  • The rules are yours. When a transaction lands in the wrong category, you fix the rule — not a support ticket.
  • Your data stays on your machine. No cloud account, no server-side copy.

Bank connections

SafeToSpend uses Plaid to read balances and transactions from accounts you explicitly connect. It requests read access only — it cannot move money, and it never sees your banking password.

Review or revoke any connection at my.plaid.com.
