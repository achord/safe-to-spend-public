How it works

  • It reads your accounts. Balances and transactions come straight from your bank, so the starting number is the real one, pending included.
  • It finds the bills. Repeating charges are identified from your own history and projected forward, with variable amounts marked as estimates rather than dressed up as facts.
  • It holds back your normal week. Groceries, gas, coffee - the spending that isn't a bill but happens anyway - averaged from your trailing 90 days.
  • What's left is yours. Move the horizon and the buffer; the number recomputes as you go.

What makes it different

  • The buffer is measured, not guessed. Most apps ask you to invent a monthly number. This one reads what you already spend.
  • It admits what it doesn't know. A variable bill is shown as a range, not a false precision. Dates are stated plainly, because dates are the reliable part.
  • The rules are yours. When something lands in the wrong category you edit the rule and it stays fixed - no support ticket, no black box.
  • Your data stays on your machine. No cloud account, no server-side copy, nothing to breach.

Bank connections

SafeToSpend uses Plaid to read balances and transactions from accounts you explicitly connect. It requests read access only - it cannot move money, and it never sees your banking password.
