Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

SafeToSpend is a personal budgeting tool. Your financial data is stored on your own computer. It is not uploaded to a server we run, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone.

Who this covers

SafeToSpend is an independently developed personal finance application (“the app”, “we”). This policy explains what the app does with information when you connect a financial account to it.

What the app accesses

When you choose to connect a bank account, the app uses Plaid Inc. to retrieve, on a read-only basis:

  • Account names, types, and the last few digits of account numbers
  • Current and available balances
  • Transaction history — date, amount, description, merchant, and pending status

The app requests read access only. It cannot initiate transfers, make payments, or change anything at your bank.

Where your data is stored

Account and transaction data retrieved by the app is written to local storage on the device where you run it. There is no SafeToSpend cloud account, no hosted database, and no server-side copy of your transactions.

The credential the app uses to fetch your data on your behalf — a Plaid access token — is stored locally on the same device and is never transmitted anywhere except to Plaid’s API when refreshing your data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or license your financial data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking, or telemetry inside the app.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, other than Plaid as described above.
  • We never see or store your online banking username or password. Those are entered directly with your bank during its own sign-in flow.

Plaid

Connecting an account means Plaid also processes your information as a data provider. Plaid’s handling of that data is governed by its own End User Privacy Policy. We encourage you to read it.

Your control

  • Revoke a bank connection at any time through my.plaid.com, or from within your bank’s own security settings. Revoking stops any further data retrieval immediately.
  • Delete your data by removing the app’s local data directory and stored token. Because everything is local, this is complete and permanent — there is no copy elsewhere for us to delete.

Data retention

The app keeps transaction history locally for as long as you keep it, so that it can compute trends and forecasts. It does not expire on a schedule. Deleting the local data removes it.

Children

SafeToSpend is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
